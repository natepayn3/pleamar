# Handoff: Live bend on the card (zerocopy-0.7), 2026-11-02

Engine half of making Vitreus's glass bend smooth. Compositor half: natepayn3/pleamar-wm branch `zerocopy-0.7`.
Vitreus side: commit 67841aa on natepayn3/Vitreus main (Glass page: "Every frame" at the far left of Live bend refresh).
Base: upstream pleamar 0.7.10 (0fdca2d). Nothing merged, nothing sent upstream.

## What is in this branch
1. **Captures on the card** (port of the Oct 30 `zerocopy-backdrop` work to 0.7.10). When the compositor offers
   `linux_dmabuf` in a wlr-screencopy frame, the render makes a buffer on the card per sheet (`CardBackdrop`,
   `dmabuf::export_to_capture`) and the compositor copies into it; no readback, no upload. Then the box is captured
   whole (no parts: `CARD_SEEN`). A failed card copy -> shared memory for 30 s. `PLEAMAR_BACKDROP_SHM=1` forces the old way.
2. **A new background beats the surface's `rate:`** (`Sheet::backdrop_fresh`): the bar keeps 12 frames a second at rest
   (Vitreus 91a5ffb, for the frame ripple), and the bend followed a window dragged under it at that pace. The pacing wait
   now also wakes for a `Watching` capture.
3. **Did what is behind change?** is judged on the card (`lens.rs Compare`): the capture is unmixed against the canvas it was
   taken with and compared to the kept background, one pixel in nine, ONLY where we draw (canvas alpha > 0.05). Raw captures
   counted our own ripple as a change (bar went 12 -> 39 paints/s at rest). Never waited for: each capture is judged by the
   card's last answer (waiting cost 10-20 ms a frame). `PLEAMAR_TIMING=1` prints "of 300 captures on the card, N changed".

## Measured (RTX 3080, Vitreus, music on)
- Card copies: Live bend at ~45 captures/s cost the same as 2/s (compositor `screen` thread 17-21% both). Before, 33 ms
  added ~18 pts on the compositor. Shell `render` ~48% (was ~58-63%).
- At rest with item 3: back to 12-13 rounds/s, 0 "changed" at rest.

## The dmabuf leak (GPU OOM 17:28): found and fixed, needs a test on the RTX 3080
- **What was seen.** With this build in every scene for ~20 min (bar restarted ~15 times, two crash loops), EVERY pleamar
  scene held 36-80 open 16 MiB `/dmabuf:` fds (normal: 6), even scenes with no glass (wptrans, cursortrail). Card hit
  9.6/10 GB, nvidia-drm "Failed to allocate NVKMS memory for GEM object", bar panicked at start.
- **Cause: inherited, not grown.** NVIDIA's `vkGetMemoryFdKHR` returns the exported fd without close-on-exec (Mesa's sets
  it), and nothing closes fds before a `run`/`spawn` or a keeper's restart. Every child kept every frame and capture of
  its parent alive on the card: Vitreus starts `vitreus-keep`, the keeper restarts scenes, each crash of the bar left
  its buffers in whatever it had spawned. Hence a different count per process, and why "stock + one restart" was clean.
- **Reproduced headless** (T600 laptop, scenes with `PLEAMAR_GPU=high`, a glass bar spawning `sleep` every 5 s): before,
  its 2 card buffers had fd flags `02` and every child held both; after, flags CLOEXEC and children hold 0 (19 checked
  over 90 s), captures still on the card.
- **Fix** (commit after 782f3c1): `dmabuf.rs` sets FD_CLOEXEC on every exported fd. And a smaller leak: a sheet that went
  (monitor asleep, popup closed) kept its `CardBackdrop` and the compositor its `wl_buffer` for the life of the process;
  now the buffer owns its `wl_buffer` (destroyed in `Drop`) and the render keeps `CARD_BACKDROPS` to live sheets each
  round. This second part is only code-checked: headless pleamar-wm cannot take a monitor away.
- **pleamar-wm** paints with this crate's `dmabuf.rs`: rebuild it against this commit too.
- **To check on the 3080:** after a while with restarts, `for p in $(pgrep -x pleamar); do ls -l /proc/$p/fd | grep -c dmabuf; done`
  stays ~6 per scene, and a scene's children (`pgrep -P PID`) hold none.

## OPEN
- **Compositor device lost in the headless rig:** zerocopy-0.7 pleamar-wm headless (Intel) with NVIDIA clients logs
  "Error in Device::poll: Parent device is lost" in its screen threads within a minute, old client build too. Not looked into.
- **Not proven:** that item 3 detects a real window moving under the glass (only "0 changed at rest" was seen).
- **Bar cut off every 1-2 min on stock 0.7.10** ("Connection reset by peer", no protocol error): wayland-rs kills a client
  silently on a request it cannot parse (unknown object). WAYLAND_DEBUG on the shell: last requests were 11
  `zwlr_screencopy_frame_v1.destroy` + 15 `capture_output_region` parts (#117). With whole-box card captures: no drops in
  10 min. The pleamar-wm branch logs "wayland · cut off a program: unreadable request on object N opcode M" (diagnostics
  commit) to name it.
- Bigger cost left: the bar recomposes its whole scene every round (rules ~4 ms + compose ~5 ms) with music on.

## Rules from the user
No Co-Authored-By in commits. Never merge without the user testing locally. Announce anything shown on screen.
One headless pleamar-wm at a time, always with PLEAMAR_SOCKETS set (killing `dbus-run-session` does not kill the
pleamar-wm under it: check `pgrep -f "[p]leamar-wm headless"`). Never put "Every frame" on with the stock engine.
